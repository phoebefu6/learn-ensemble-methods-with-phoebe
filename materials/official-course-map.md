**Course:** Ensemble Methods: XGBoost & LightGBM - the tabular champions. Bagging, boosting, and tuning gradient-boosted trees that win in practice.
**Arc:** single-track builder 6 sessions, running project: predict churn on **Mango Lane** (fashion app, ecom canon: AOV $68, monthly GMV $660,960, 12,000 customers; labs run on the deterministic 300-customer sample - 200 train / 100 holdout).

## The simulator: ensemble-live.js (canon verified in-engine 2026-09-03)

REAL ensemble learning in the browser - genuine greedy split-search trees fitted live to the embedded 300-customer Mango Lane churn sample (seeded deterministic, 10% label noise, nonlinear signal with two interactions). 200 train / 100 holdout. Two mounts: `#ensemble-watch` (session 3 - watch boosting learn, +10 trees at a time, train/holdout curves, levers depth 1/3/6 and lr 0.5/0.1) and `#ensemble-tune` (session 6 - five-config leaderboard, each fits its whole ensemble live on click).

Canon (train / holdout accuracy, %):

| Config | Train | Holdout | Teaching beat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single tree, depth 6 | 75.0 | 58.0 | one tree memorizes fragments, generalizes worst |
| Bagged forest, 60 trees d6, feature subsample | 88.5 | 68.0 | variance averaged away: +10 holdout |
| Boosting untuned, d6 lr .5, 60 rounds | 100 | 68.0 | ties the forest while train screams 100 - the gap is 32 |
| Boosting tuned, d3 lr .1, 200 rounds | 100 | **73.0** | ESL's small-v-many-M strategy, measured live |
| ⚠ More-trees-forever, d6 lr .5, 600 rounds | 100 | 70.0 | 540 extra trees buy nothing - anti-lever |
| (reference) stumps d1 lr .1, 200 | 72.0 | 68.0 | stumps generalize but cap - depth is a real dial |

Watch-mode bonus (real, not scripted): at d3 lr .1 the holdout curve PEAKS at 77% at round 48 and drifts to 73 by round 200 (browser-verified) - early stopping teaches itself from the live curve (pairs with ESL: choose M by early stopping).

## Hard rails / honesty

- The simulator is REAL: real trees, real splits, measured accuracy - say so on the widget. The only artifice is the seeded dataset.
- Plain accuracy used deliberately (balanced 48/47% churn sample); metric design lives in learn-model-evaluation - one pointer, no re-teach.
- Never "17 of 29 competitions" - blog-published winning solutions. Never "LightGBM 20x faster than XGBoost" - vs pre-sorted conventional GBDT.
- TabPFN taught as the honest challenger: Nature-verified on <=10K samples, vendor-claimed at 2.5 scale, inference cost real, and zero appearances in 2024 competition winners. Both sides, dated.
- The in-browser engine is a teaching implementation (quantile splits, 8 candidate thresholds, no regularization terms beyond Newton leaves) - it demonstrates the MECHANISM; xgboost/lightgbm ship far more (say so in s4).
